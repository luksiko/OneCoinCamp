Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about 1€ camper rentals and automated alerts.

Is it really 1€? Are there any hidden catches? +
Yes! The base rental price is exactly 1€ per day. Rental companies charge this nominal amount to enter a binding rental contract. You receive a fully insured, brand-new campervan or motorhome. The only condition is following the designated route and dates.
Who pays for fuel and highway tolls? +
Many relocation deals include generous fuel subsidies (usually between €100 and €300). You submit your gas receipts upon returning the vehicle, and the rental company refunds you directly. Highway tolls are paid by the driver.
What driver's license do I need? +
98% of campervans and motorhomes in Europe are under 3,500 kg, meaning a standard passenger car license (Category B in Europe, or regular US/UK driver's license) held for at least 1–2 years is completely sufficient.
